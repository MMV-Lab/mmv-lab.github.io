Davide obtained his Ph.D. in Physics (cum laude) in 2025 from the [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://en.unimib.it/) under the supervision of Prof. Laura Sironi and Prof. Giuseppe Chirico. His doctoral work, funded by the EU Horizon 2020 FET project [IN2SIGHT](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/964481), focused on AI-powered digital pathology and non-linear microscopy for the diagnosis and quantification of fibrosis, in close collaboration with clinical pathologists ([Humanitas University Hospital](https://www.humanitas.net/), [San Raffaele Hospital](https://research.hsr.it/en/index.html)). Before that, he earned his B.Sc. and M.Sc. (cum laude) in Physics at the same university, where phasor analysis of spectral images became a thread that runs through all of his work. He joined the AMBIOM group at ISAS in April 2025 as a postdoctoral researcher.

Since June 2026, Davide is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at ISAS, working on physics-aware generative AI for spectral microscopy. His broader interests include generative AI for microscopy, virtual staining, digital pathology, and FAIR microscopy data management. At ISAS he also builds data portals based on [BioFile Finder](https://bff.allencell.org/). He has worked with a wide range of imaging modalities, including confocal, two-photon, SHG, FLIM and STED.
