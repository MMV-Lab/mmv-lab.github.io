Lukas Johanns completed his Bachelor's degree in Physics at RWTH Aachen and his Master's in Medical Physics at TU Dortmund specializing in medical imaging and machine learning. 

Lukas is pursuing his PhD in the AMBIOM group at ISAS focussing on developing Agentic-J; an AI agent for bioimage analysis. The goal of this project is to make bioimage analysis more accessible to biologists, giving them the ability to learn image analysis and plan/execute processing pipelines in cooperation with an AI helper.
