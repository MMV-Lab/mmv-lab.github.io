# <i class="fa-solid fa-wand-magic-sparkles"></i> Learn with AI

<p class="lw-lead">AI is changing what learning can look like.</p>

A concept that used to take a textbook chapter and a patient teacher can now become a small interactive page: move a slider, press play, watch what happens, and build the intuition yourself. This page collects the web apps we have created together with AI to explain ideas in AI, microscopy, multi-omics, biology, and medicine, plus a few made purely for fun. Every app runs directly in your browser, with nothing to install and no account needed. Pick a topic and start exploring. And if you spot a mistake or have an idea for a new app, [let us know]({{ "/contact/" | relative_url }}).
