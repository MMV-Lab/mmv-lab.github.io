给家长 · For parents

这个页面用德语写成，按德语版 PowerPoint 的菜单名称讲解。它不是按钮大全，而是带孩子走一遍做演示文稿的思路：先在纸上想清楚（听众、一句话核心信息、顺序），再搭框架、写要点、配图、统一设计、克制地用动画，最后练习和上台。黄色光圈表示“点这里”，右侧卡片解释“为什么”。每站结尾有一个小任务，右上角还有“按钮测验”。贯穿全程的例子是一份关于章鱼的口头报告。

This page is in German and uses the menu names of the German PowerPoint. It is not a button catalogue: it walks your child through the way of thinking behind a good deck. Plan on paper first (audience, one-sentence message, order), then build the skeleton, write keywords, add a picture, keep one design, use animation sparingly, and finally rehearse and present. The yellow ring shows where to click, the card explains why. Every station ends with a small task, and the button quiz sits at the top right. The running example is a school talk about the octopus.
